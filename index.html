❓ Help & Controls

Turn

  • Tap or drag the map to aim; hold FIRE to charge rockets and lobs, release to shoot — other weapons fire on tap.
  • Walk with ◀ ▶; adjust aim with ▲ ▼.
  • ⤒ jumps — tap it twice quickly to backflip (hop backwards with a somersault); tap once more mid-flip for a flat low backflip.
  • 🚀 jetpack — hold to fly; fuel drains fast and refills every turn. CPU teams fly too.
  • Pinch (or scroll) to zoom — the camera follows the active worm and any shell in flight. Two-finger drag (or right-drag) pans the map; move or fire to snap back.
  • Tap one of your other worms to take control of it (turn Worm Select off in 🎨 Customize to disable).
  • After firing you get a short retreat window; press FIRE to skip it.

Weapons

  • ⇄ flips the weapon shelf page; keys 1–7 pick on-page.
  • ✈️ Airstrike, 🌀 Teleport and 🌩️ Lightning: tap the map to pick a spot, then FIRE.
  • 🐑 Sheep walks on its own — press FIRE again to detonate it. 🐉 Dragon Ball bounces wildly; press FIRE to set it off (it also blows after six bounces) with a huge knockback.
  • 🐄 Mad Cow — unleash three charging cows one after another; each explodes on the first worm (friend or foe) or wall it meets, with Dynamite-strength force.
  • 🍌 Banana Bomb erupts into five bananas that burst in sequence; 🦫 Mole tunnels through rock in the direction you fired it, then detonates when it comes out.
  • 🔴 Mines arm after 2 s, stay between turns, and chain-react when shot.
  • 🔥 Blowtorch cuts a tunnel; 😇 Holy Grenade has the biggest blast.
  • 🛸 Flying Saucer: press FIRE to board it for the turn — fly with 🚀 and the walk pad (fuel-limited, works underwater); press FIRE again to land.
  • 🛰️ Homing Missile: tap the map to mark a target, then hold FIRE to charge it like a bazooka — it locks on and homes in, but crashes into anything in the way; ⛏️ Bunker Buster drills a three-blast tunnel through the ground; 🪄 Magic Bullet (one per team) spirals a path to the mark and erupts with a Holy-Grenade-sized blast.
  • Weapons with no ammo left vanish from the shelf — they reappear when you have ammo again.

Keyboard

  • Arrows / WASD move & aim · Space fire · Enter / Shift jump
  • 1–7 weapon · Q shelf page · J jetpack · +/− zoom · P / Esc pause · H help
  • On a desktop computer the touch pads hide automatically — play with the mouse (aim, wheel to zoom) and keyboard.

Schemes & Options

  • Normal: standard ammo. Greedy: every weapon, unlimited ammo. Pro Mode: scarce ammo, no airstrike. Bazooka Only: just rockets.
  • The start menu sets teams (2–4), worms each, HP, turn time, wind, crates and map size; the 🎨 Customize screen picks team names, sky, terrain, weather, graphics and sound.
  • Map size: Standard, Wide, or Epic — bigger maps keep large armies from cluttering. Islands splits the map into up to six floating sky islands.

Tips

  • The wind meter pushes rockets and rain — check it every turn.
  • After turn 16 the sea rises, worms wither, and meteors fall.
  • Parachuted crates give +HP or bonus ammo.
  • Pick Low graphics in the 🎨 Customize screen for smoother play on older phones.
  • Phones and tablets: play in landscape — portrait shows a rotate reminder.
