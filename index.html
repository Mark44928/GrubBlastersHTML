🐛 Grub Blasters

Turn-based worm artillery. Blast the other team into the sea!

Tap or drag the map to aim · hold FIRE to charge · double-tap jump to backflip!

❓ Help & Controls

Turn

  • Tap or drag the map to aim; hold FIRE to charge rockets and lobs, release to shoot — other weapons fire on tap.
  • Walk with ◀ ▶; adjust aim with ▲ ▼. Aimless weapons (dynamite, mines, animals, punches and utilities) only use the worm's facing — a sideways tap turns the worm; ▲▼ do nothing.
  • ⤒ jumps — tap it twice quickly to backflip (hop backwards with a somersault); tap once more mid-flip for a flat low backflip.
  • 🚀 jetpack — hold to fly; fuel drains fast and refills every turn. CPU teams fly too. The jetpack, flying saucer and parachute are movement utilities — using them does not end your turn.
  • Pinch (or scroll) to zoom — the camera follows the active worm and any shell in flight. Two-finger drag (or right-drag) pans the map; move or fire to snap back.
  • Tap one of your other worms to take control of it (turn Worm Select off in ⚙️ Settings to disable).
  • After firing you get a short retreat window; press FIRE to skip it. Use 🎬 (or R) to replay the last shot — FIRE cycles play / 3× / pause, S slow-mo, Esc/Enter closes; Auto Replay is in ⚙️ Settings.
  • HUD buttons: 🎬 replay · 🔄 restart this map · ⏸ pause · ⚙️ Settings · ✕ quit to the menu.

Weapons

  • Pick a weapon from the weapon shelf in the bottom bar — tap it to open the full list; keys 1–7 select the first seven.
  • ✈️ Airstrike, 🌀 Teleport, 🌩️ Lightning, 🛩️ Carpet Bomb and ☢️ Nuke: tap the map to pick a spot, then FIRE — the carpet strafes the mark and the nuke sounds a siren before the game's biggest blast. 🕊️ Pigeon: mark a spot and it flies straight through terrain to reach it.
  • 🐑 Sheep walks on its own — press FIRE again to detonate it. 🐉 Dragon Ball bounces wildly; press FIRE to set it off (it also blows after six bounces) with a huge knockback.
  • 🐄 Mad Cows — unleash three charging cows one after another; each explodes on the first worm (friend or foe) or wall it meets, with Dynamite-strength force.
  • 🍌 Banana Bomb erupts into five bananas that burst in sequence; 🦫 Mole tunnels through rock in the direction you fired it, then detonates when it comes out.
  • 🔴 Mines arm after 2 s, stay between turns, and chain-react when shot.
  • 🔥 Blowtorch cuts a tunnel; 😇 Holy Grenade packs a huge blast.
  • 🛸 Flying Saucer: press FIRE to board it for the turn — fly with 🚀 and the walk pad (fuel-limited, works underwater); press FIRE again to land.
  • 🛰️ Homing Missile: tap the map to mark a target, then hold FIRE to charge it like a bazooka — it locks on and homes in, but crashes into anything in the way; ⛏️ Bunker Buster drills a three-blast tunnel through the ground; 🪄 Magic Bullet (one per team) spirals a path to the mark and erupts with a Holy-Grenade-sized blast.
  • ⚙️ Minigun hoses a rapid spray of sixteen bullets; 🎌 Kamikaze launches your worm on a suicidal dig-through-everything charge that detonates on contact; 💪 1.5x Damage boosts all damage and knockback you deal for the rest of the turn.
  • 🔥 Fire Punch is an uppercut that knocks a nearby enemy straight up into the air (and carves through terrain) — useful for launching worms into mines or water; 🛡️ Invulnerable shields the active worm in a bubble for the rest of the turn — immune to damage, fall damage and poison, but NOT drowning or knockback.
  • 🪂 Parachute leaps into a gentle glide — steer with ◀ ▶, slow or speed the descent with ▲ ▼, and land without fall damage; the turn continues, so switch weapons and fire after landing. 👉 Prod gives a worm a harmless shove (ideal for nudging one into the sea); 💀 Suicide Bomber sacrifices your worm to release a poison cloud — the blast deals 30 damage like Worms Armageddon; ⏭️ Skip ends your turn on the spot and the enemy calls you a coward (and it never runs out).
  • 🔥 Napalm bursts into burning ground; 💥 Dyn. Bundle pops open into three bouncing dynamite sticks; 🦨 Skunk collects crates, then sprays a wind-drifted poison cloud — press FIRE to spray it early and again to detonate it.
  • 🛢️ Explosive barrels are solid — stand on them or drop behind one; shoot it and it erupts, chaining into mines and other barrels. About one in three is an oil drum: its blast leaves burning ground. They fall when the ground beneath them is blown away.
  • 🛡️ Shield raises a bubble that absorbs your next hit — damage, fall damage or poison; 🌍 Earthquake shakes every grounded enemy off its feet; 🧛 Vampire drains 35 HP from a nearby enemy and heals you by the same amount; 🟢 Sticky Bomb sticks to the first surface it touches; 🟡 Rubber Grenade ricochets off everything until its 4 s fuse runs out; 🪵 Girder drops a solid platform; 🫏 Concrete Donkey is dropped from the sky onto a spot you mark — tap the map, then FIRE; 🧲 Crate Vacuum drags every supply crate toward you.
  • Out-of-ammo weapons stay in the shelf but grey out with ×0 — they light up again when you have ammo.

Keyboard

  • Arrows / WASD move & aim · Space fire · Enter / Shift jump
  • 1–7 weapon · J jetpack · R replay · S slow-mo (replay) · +/− zoom · P / Esc pause · H help
  • Esc closes Help, Customize, Settings and the scheme editor; Esc/Enter also exits a replay.
  • On a desktop computer the touch pads hide automatically — play with the mouse (aim, wheel to zoom) and keyboard.

Schemes & Settings

  • Normal: standard ammo. Greedy: every weapon, unlimited ammo. Pro Mode: scarce ammo, no airstrike. Bazooka Only: just rockets.
  • Tap 🔨 next to Weapon Scheme to build a custom loadout — press ＋ for a new scheme, set each weapon to ∞ or 0–10, then Save; schemes are saved on the device and can be downloaded/imported as JSON. Deleting the last scheme leaves the editor empty until you press ＋ again.
  • 🎨 Customize sets the match: teams, worms per team, HP, turn time, computer level, scheme, gravity, sudden death, retreat time, jetpack fuel, wind, crates, barrels, map size, islands, aim guide, team names/colors, sky, terrain and weather.
  • ⚙️ Settings (menu, or the gear in the HUD) covers interface theme (Modern / Classic / Gruvbox / Tokyo Night / Dark / Nord / OLED Dark / Everforest), sound, menu song, vibration, auto replay, Worm Select, crate radar, graphics and fullscreen.
  • Map size: Standard, Wide, or Epic — bigger maps keep large armies from cluttering. Islands splits the map into up to six floating sky islands.

Tips

  • The wind meter pushes rockets and rain — check it every turn.
  • Sudden death: Standard raises the sea from turn 16, Late from turn 25, Off never — then worms wither and meteors fall.
  • Parachuted crates give +HP or bonus ammo.
  • Fires from napalm and oil drums keep damaging worms standing in them; health crates also cure ☠ poison.
  • Pick Low graphics in ⚙️ Settings for smoother play on older phones.
  • Phones and tablets: play in landscape — portrait shows a rotate reminder.
