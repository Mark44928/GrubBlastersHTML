🐛 Grub Blasters

Turn-based worm artillery. Blast the other team into the sea!

Tap or drag the map to aim · hold FIRE to charge · double-tap jump to backflip!

❓ Help & Controls

Turn

  • Tap or drag the map to aim; hold FIRE to charge rockets and lobs, release to shoot — other weapons fire on tap.
  • Walk with ◀ ▶; adjust aim with ▲ ▼. Aimless weapons (dynamite, mines, animals, punches and utilities) only use the worm's facing — a sideways tap turns the worm; ▲▼ do nothing.
  • ⤒ jumps — tap it twice quickly to backflip (hop backwards with a somersault); tap once more mid-flip for a flat low backflip.
  • 🚀 jetpack — hold to fly; fuel drains fast and refills every turn. CPU teams fly too. The jetpack, flying saucer and parachute are movement utilities — using them does not end your turn.
  • Pinch (or scroll) to zoom — the camera follows the active worm and any shell in flight. Two-finger drag (or right-drag) pans the map; move or fire to snap back.
  • Tap one of your other worms to take control of it (turn Worm Select off in ⚙️ Settings to disable).
  • After firing you get a short retreat window; press FIRE to skip it. Use 🎬 (or R) to replay the last shot — FIRE cycles play / 3× / pause, S slow-mo, Esc/Enter closes; Auto Replay is in ⚙️ Settings.
  • HUD buttons: 📷 screenshot · 🎬 replay · 🔄 restart this map · ⏸ pause · ⚙️ Settings · ✕ quit to the menu.

Weapons

  • Pick a weapon from the weapon shelf in the bottom bar — tap it to open the full list; keys 1–7 select the first seven.
  • 🚁 Major Munch: mark a spot, then press FIRE — Munch rides in on the Grubheli, drops one Grenade on the mark and flies off. One per team per match. ✈️ Airstrike, 🌠 Halo Strike, 🌀 Teleport, 🌩️ Lightning, 🛩️ Carpet Bomb and ☢️ Nuke: tap the map to pick a spot, then FIRE — the carpet strafes the mark and the nuke sounds a siren before the game's biggest blast. 🐝 Hornet: mark a spot and it flies straight through terrain to reach it.
  • 🪲 Bouncer Bug walks on its own — press FIRE again to detonate it. ☄️ Comet Fist bounces wildly; press FIRE to set it off (it also blows after six bounces) with a huge knockback.
  • 🐛 Feral Grubs — unleash three charging grubs one after another; each explodes on the first grub (friend or foe) or wall it meets, with Dynamite-strength force.
  • 🐃 Grub of Lies — a full-on Buffalo of Lies riff: charges forward in the direction you face, steps over gentle terrain, detonates dead-on into steep walls and carves through, then vanishes after six bursts (32 damage each); collects crates it runs over.
  • 🥭 Mango Bomb erupts into five mangoes that burst in sequence; 🕳️ Burrow Bomb tunnels through rock in the direction you fired it, then detonates when it comes out.
  • 🔴 Mines arm after 2 s, stay between turns, and chain-react when shot.
  • 🔥 Blowtorch cuts a tunnel; ✨ Halo Grenade packs a huge blast.
  • 🛸 Flying Saucer: press FIRE to board it for the turn — fly with 🚀 and the walk pad (fuel-limited, works underwater); press FIRE again to land.
  • 🚀 Homing Missile: tap the map to mark a target, then hold FIRE to charge it like a bazooka — it locks on and homes in, but crashes into anything in the way; ⛏️ Bunker Buster drills a three-blast tunnel through the ground; 🎯 Seeker Round (one per team) spirals a path to the mark and erupts with a Halo-Grenade-sized blast.
  • 🔫 Minigun hoses a rapid spray of sixteen bullets; 💣 Kamikaze launches your worm on a suicidal dig-through-everything charge that detonates on contact; 💪 1.5x Damage boosts all damage and knockback you deal for the rest of the turn.
  • 🥊 Blaze Uppercut is an uppercut that knocks a nearby enemy straight up into the air (and carves through terrain) — useful for launching worms into mines or water; 🔮 Invulnerable shields the active worm in a bubble for the rest of the turn — immune to damage, fall damage and poison, but NOT drowning or knockback.
  • 🪂 Parachute leaps into a gentle glide — steer with ◀ ▶, slow or speed the descent with ▲ ▼, and land without fall damage; the turn continues, so switch weapons and fire after landing. 👉 Prod gives a worm a harmless shove (ideal for nudging one into the sea); 💀 Suicide Bomber sacrifices your worm to release a poison cloud — the blast deals 30 damage like classic artillery; ⏭️ Skip ends your turn on the spot and the enemy calls you a coward (and it never runs out).
  • 🔥 Napalm bursts into burning ground; 💥 Dyn. Bundle pops open into three bouncing dynamite sticks; 🪳 Stinkbug collects crates, then sprays a wind-drifted poison cloud — press FIRE to spray it early and again to detonate it.
  • 🚁 Major Munch: mark a spot, then hold and release FIRE — the Grubheli flies over, Munch drops the crew grenade, and he rides off again. One per team per match; the Grubsong will play whether you win or not.
  • 🛢️ Explosive barrels are solid — stand on them or drop behind one; shoot it and it erupts, chaining into mines and other barrels. About one in three is an oil drum: its blast leaves burning ground. They fall when the ground beneath them is blown away.
  • 🛡️ Shield raises a bubble that absorbs your next hit — damage, fall damage or poison; 🌍 Earthquake shakes every grounded enemy off its feet; 🧛 Vampire drains 35 HP from a nearby enemy and heals you by the same amount; 🟢 Sticky Bomb sticks to the first surface it touches; 🟡 Rubber Grenade ricochets off everything until its 4 s fuse runs out; 🪵 Girder drops a solid platform; 🪨 Granite Grub is dropped from the sky onto a spot you mark — tap the map, then FIRE; 📦 Crate Vacuum drags every supply crate toward you.
  • Out-of-ammo weapons stay in the shelf but grey out with ×0 — they light up again when you have ammo.

Keyboard

  • Arrows / WASD move & aim · Space fire · Enter / Shift jump
  • 1–7 weapon · J jetpack · R replay · S slow-mo (replay) · +/− zoom · P / Esc pause · H help
  • Esc closes Help, Customize, Settings and the scheme editor; Esc/Enter also exits a replay.
  • On a desktop computer the touch pads hide automatically — play with the mouse (aim, wheel to zoom) and keyboard.

Schemes & Settings

  • Normal: standard ammo. Greedy: every weapon, unlimited ammo. Pro Mode: scarce ammo, no airstrike. Bazooka Only: just rockets.
  • Tap 🔨 next to Weapon Scheme to build a custom loadout — press ＋ for a new scheme, set each weapon to ∞ or 0–10, then Save; schemes are saved on the device and can be downloaded/imported as JSON. Deleting the last scheme leaves the editor empty until you press ＋ again.
  • 🎨 Customize sets the match: teams, worms per team, HP, turn time, computer level, scheme, gravity, sudden death, retreat time, jetpack fuel, wind, crates, barrels, map size, islands, aim guide, team names/colors, sky, terrain and weather.
  • ⚙️ Settings (menu, or the gear in the HUD) covers interface theme (Modern / Classic / Gruvbox / Tokyo Night / Dark / Nord / OLED Dark / Everforest / Greenit / Bluney / Yellen), sound, menu song, vibration, motion (Full / Low / Off — screen-shake intensity), auto replay, Worm Select, crate radar, graphics and fullscreen.
  • Map size: Standard, Wide, or Epic — bigger maps keep large armies from cluttering. Islands splits the map into up to six floating sky islands.

Tips

  • The wind meter pushes rockets and rain — check it every turn.
  • Sudden death: Standard raises the sea from turn 16, Late from turn 25, Off never — then worms wither and meteors fall.
  • Parachuted crates give +HP or bonus ammo.
  • Fires from napalm and oil drums keep damaging worms standing in them; health crates also cure ☠ poison.
  • Pick Low graphics in ⚙️ Settings for smoother play on older phones.
  • Phones and tablets: play in landscape — portrait shows a rotate reminder.

📚 Weapon Codex

📖 The Grubsong

Grub Blasters & Major Munch — field notes for Grubsong ’26

1. The Dug-out. In the year 2026 a broke band of grubs — soap-stained and aiming at the clouds — dug their home beneath a single crusty oak. They called it the Dug-out, and every grub in it had already lost someone to the long war on the Ridge.

2. Major Munch. Their youngest ace: a grub with a pilot’s licence, a dangerous left hand and one rule — one grenade, one drop zone, no repeats. He flew the Grubheli with a tail rotor he’d tuned himself, and the Ridge called him Major Munch because he never left a crew behind.

3. The Mascot. Three inches of grub with a pilot’s swagger, one enormous tooth, and an appetite that once emptied the mess tin before breakfast. Sergeant Munch piloted the Grubheli with his knees, his left hand, and whatever was left in the ration crate. He never volunteered; he volunteered the whole crew.

4. The Drop Zone. The Ridge held a thousand barrels and one very smug garrison. Munch primed one grenade and held the heli steady as they rode the wind in low. One drop ended the Siege of the Crusty Oak.

5. The Anthem. They didn’t carve statues; they wrote a song. Not to win — to remember how to fight back on a Tuesday morning. It goes: grubs hold the line, we sing it till the walls come down…

6. Grubsong ’26. The melody and words are original to Grub Blasters. Major Munch is our own mascot, drawn for this game, and every grub in the crew is ours — no borrowed heroes, no borrowed songs.

Whether you win or lose: play one round for the Grub. In memoriam: every grub that made it back.
